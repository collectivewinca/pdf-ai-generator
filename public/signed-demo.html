How It Works
From prompt to signed document
01

Describe your document

Tell the AI what you need: "Create a signed business letter to Apple about an AI partnership"

02

AI generates the spec

Gemini Pro creates a structured document spec with a __SIGNATURE_PLACEHOLDER__ where the signature belongs.

03

Signature renders + embeds

The server renders the animated signature as a PNG via ve-animesign and embeds it in the final PDF.
